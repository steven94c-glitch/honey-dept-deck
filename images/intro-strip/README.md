# intro-strip/

**Shows up:** the horizontal scrolling marquee directly after the "Hello." intro page (just before Chapter 01: Approach & Tone).

**Slots:** 8 images, named `01.jpg` through `08.jpg`. They scroll continuously and loop. Order matters — `01` is leftmost.

**Aspect:** wide (each frame renders at roughly 28vw × 32vh — closer to 16:10).

**Vibe:** sets the documentary-cinematic tone of the whole deck. Mix of references — observational portraits, deadpan tableaux, Andersson stills, Tati stills, anything that lands the moodboard.
