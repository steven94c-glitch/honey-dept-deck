Demographics

Honey Dept. is made for anyone and everyone. It's made to eat anywhere doing anything. Our demographic is specifically anyone over the age of one.

Crossing guards eat HD. Manicurists eat HD. Foley artists, nuns, auctioneers, third-generation taxidermists, puppeteers, and dog groomers eat HD.

This is a series of portraits or films about specific average people living their lives and eating honey. Fly on the wall, the honey is just another character. We're focused on the eater eating, everyone around them goes about their business.

Think: John Wilson, Bill Owens, Martin Parr, Vernon, Florida

Moodboard

The references are Roy Andersson, Martin Parr, Bill Owens, Errol Morris, Jacques Tati — filmmakers and photographers with genuine curiosity about ordinary people in ordinary situations, and the patience to let those situations be exactly what they are.

The humor is always there, and it's never mean. Deadpan, warm, specific. Nobody is the punchline. The product is present but not celebrated. Every frame should look like it could be from a film that has nothing to do with honey.

Titles

Let's not let framing get in the way of titles.

Each film cuts to a dedicated title frame at the end. The frame isolates the subject with a crop, circular to mimic the circles ("bees") used in branding. The super can appear above, below or beside the image, depending on the crop.

The title frame replaces the need for in-scene headroom or negative space. Framing during production stays unconstrained.

Eat It Anywhere

Eat it near a horse. Eat it in a stairwell you don't belong in. Eat it in front of someone jogging. Eat it on the wrong train. Eat it while holding something fragile. Eat it walking up the down escalator. Eat it inside your coat, quietly. Eat it while whispering to a plant. Eat it mid-apology. Eat it behind the shed, where no one thinks to look. Eat it in the good chair, the one they said not to use. Eat it during the group photo, just slightly out of frame.

This honey is the first honey that can be eaten anywhere. So eat it wherever you want doing whatever you're doing. It's your honey.

An edit of short vignettes. Locked-off shots. One scenario, one take, cut. The titles and VO run over them.

A Note From Our Founder

Every company has a founder. Ours has several.

A series of films in which a rotating cast of individuals — a bodega owner, a seven-year-old girl, someone's grandmother, a drag queen — each sit at a desk and deliver an official communication from Honey Department. A press release. A response to customer feedback. An AMA. Q3 results. An apology. A product announcement.

The desk is the same. The uniform is the same. The founder is never the same.

Goes With Everything

Honey Department pairs well with Ricotta toast. It also goes with leather pants, climate anxiety, translating cat, corporate dystopia, overpriced candles, 2am Wikipedia spirals, motel ice, amicable divorces, trying to appreciate jazz, New Jersey suntanning, impatiently waiting, mid-range crystal energy, sorting through garage boxes, gas station sunglasses shopping, post office parking lots, fake accents, hotel stationery, benign neglect, discount astrology, long voicemails, and timeshare scams.

Is this a food pairing guide or compatibility study? Yes. A series of simple vignettes where the honey is being enjoyed in a different scenario with the specific pairing written out.
